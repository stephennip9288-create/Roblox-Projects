## Implemented gameplay

- **The Hunt:** Hound pack; one Alpha maximum. Charger telegraphs lock a location for sidestepping; flankers use separate clear approaches, abandon stalled offsets, and return to native pursuit when obstructed. Alpha proximity influences target focus and pack pace. Flanker participation and offset distance increase with rounds.
- **Redline:** Enraged runners/shamblers with raised clavicle silhouette, torn restraint, eyes, faster gait, speed and attack cadence. Precision hits interrupt attacks with a cooldown.
- **Iron March:** Separate helmet and chest plate health. Queryable plate geometry, rivets/guards, break feedback and exposed seams communicate defense. Broken armor stays broken through navigation recovery. Ordinary uncovered regions remain viable targets.
- **Containment Breach:** Mixed runners/zombies/hounds, with one rare warned Stalker at most. Stalkers prefer isolated living operators, seek nearby cover where available, and use a warned, time-limited ambush with a long cooldown. They remain visible and cannot attack during preparation/cooldown.
- **Critical Mass:** Volatile zombies/crawlers with pressure tanks, cage, and targetable core. Destroying the reactor triggers elimination and a delayed blast. Chain deaths always receive a new complete warning.
- **Ironbound miniboss:** Extremely rare Armored + Enraged combined elite; at most one per wave, late-round gate, slightly larger silhouette, capped damage. No triple modifiers or Warden modifiers.
- Every new variant uses the existing world-colliding, non-pinning ragdoll path. Alive collision and authoritative hitboxes remain intact, including larger Alpha/combined rigs.

## Balance/configuration

All tuning lives in `dev/Source/Runtime/shared/Config.lua`, under `C.Evolution`. Scheduling/composition/stat policy is in the existing `DirectorRules` module. Ordinary base stat curves are preserved.

| --- | --- |

| Event preparation | 7 seconds; no early enemy spawning |

| Ordinary elite rate | Starts round 6 at 3.5%; +0.6 percentage points/round; cap 22% |

| Ordinary Stalker | Round 12+; 1.2% roll; maximum one per wave |

| Armor | Two plates at 32% of enemy max health each; 72% resistance on covered hits; exposed seam bonus 25% |
| Precision interrupt | 12% max-health hit; 0.45-second stagger; 2-second cooldown |
| Hound death blast | 1.25-second fuse, 7-stud radius, 20 + 0.35/round damage capped at 30 before mode scaling |
| Volatile blast | 1.6-second fuse, 9-stud radius, 28 + 0.4/round damage capped at 40 before mode scaling |
| Hazard limits | Eight active hazards; excess deaths fizzle without hidden damage; LOS checks; existing player enemy-hit grace |
| Obstruction | Smoke 2.2–2.6 seconds; opacity 26–30%; 2 low-effects or 5 normal smoke particles per hazard |

Blast damage follows existing mode scaling. Counter fields affect both existing hazards and hazards created during their active duration. Ventilation clears smoke; suppression clears flames. Neither silently removes an advertised blast. Active arc fences also interrupt elites with a cooldown; Warden phase caps remain unchanged.
