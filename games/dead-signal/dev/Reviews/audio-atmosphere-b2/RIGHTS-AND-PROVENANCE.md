# Music provenance and pending approvals

The ten delivered musical candidates are original deterministic compositions rendered by `prepare_music.py` for this task. They use additive oscillators and authored envelopes; the generator has no sample inputs, downloaded audio, sampled voices or third-party music. ffmpeg encodes the generated PCM as Ogg Vorbis. The generator and track parameters are included for inspection. No runtime synthesis is added to the game.

`MUSIC-RECORDINGS.json` records exact WAV/PCM/OGG hashes for this delivered snapshot, channel/rate/duration, decoded 4× peak estimates, clipped PCM counts and loop boundary deltas. PCM output is deterministic for the recorded generator parameters. OGG container/encoder output may vary across reruns; use the delivered file hash or explicitly record a new approved rendition. Codec checks demonstrate valid files and headroom, not artistic suitability or seamless native Roblox playback.

These are synthesized **musical** candidates, not physical recordings. They do not fill the six metal/steam/impact/cry/breath/drag requests. No marketplace asset ID, external sample license, public-domain dedication or owner approval is assumed. The owner must review creator/rights status and accept individual candidates, upload through the intended creator account/group, obtain moderation and verify each experience grant. All supplied approvals/access flags are false and asset IDs are empty. No clip is activated in the game.

For additional recordings, retain performer/source identity and a written ownership or license record allowing the planned Roblox use and editing. Do not use scraped game/movie/music audio or assume an asset is permitted merely because an ID can be found. MAIN records the accepted file hash, source/license, uploader, asset ID, moderation state, per-universe permission evidence and native audition result as separate facts.

Prior accepted Batch 1 recordings and the existing staging/production permission records remain unchanged. The new candidates do not inherit those permissions.
